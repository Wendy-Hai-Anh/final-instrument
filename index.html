Garden of Sounds

Turn your phone sideways for a bigger book.

For grown-ups

How to play

  • Tap the book to open it. Turn the pages with the big arrows or a swipe.
  • Tap a garden picture to step inside.
  • Every object in a garden is part of one musical instrument; a little gold note bubble marks each one until it has played. One tap starts an object singing a short tune from its own set of notes, made new on every tap, and another tap stops it. Dragging an object and letting go starts it too. A singing object glows and sends up little notes.
  • All objects keep the same beat and the same chords, so anything your child plays together sounds like music.
  • The play button lets the whole garden sing, one object after another, and then stops it all. The question-mark button starts a short guide your child plays along with: tap the animal, then drag it up or down and to the right. It starts by itself each time you open a garden.
  • The < and > buttons on the sides of a garden lead to the other gardens.
  • Several fingers can play at once, so siblings can play together.
  • Move an object higher for higher notes.
  • Move it further right for faster notes, and left for slower ones.

What children explore

Made for children aged 1 to 3 with a grown-up nearby. Children discover cause and effect, high and low sounds, and fast and slow rhythms through free play. There are no scores, timers or right answers: every arrangement sounds good, so children play for the fun of it.

Safe by design

No ads, no purchases and no sign-up. The toy does not ask for or keep any personal information. Keep the volume low and take breaks together.
