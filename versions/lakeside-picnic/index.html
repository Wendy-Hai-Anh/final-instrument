Garden of Sounds

Lakeside picnic garden

A spring meadow beside a calm lake, with a big pink blossom tree on the right and a pink gingham blanket on the grass. Gardening tools rest by the tree, a bird sits on its branch, a butterfly visits a flowering bush, a snail rests on a boulder and fruit lies around the picnic basket.

Tap an object to start or stop its song, and drag it to move it; it sings from wherever it is put down. Objects higher up sing higher notes, and objects further right sing faster. The play button lets the whole garden sing and stops it again, and the help button starts a short guide.

Turn your phone sideways to play.
