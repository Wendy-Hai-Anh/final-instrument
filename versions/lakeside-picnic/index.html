Garden of Sounds

Lakeside picnic garden

A spring meadow beside a calm lake, with a big pink blossom tree on the right and a pink gingham blanket on the grass. Gardening tools rest by the tree, a bird sits on its branch, a butterfly visits a flowering bush, a snail rests on a boulder and fruit lies around the picnic basket.

Tap an object to start or stop its sound, and drag it to move it. Objects higher in the garden play higher notes, and objects further right repeat faster. The pause button pauses every sound and then turns into a play button that brings them back, and the help button shows how it works.
