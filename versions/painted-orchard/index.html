Garden of Sounds

Painted orchard garden

A golden, painted orchard under a warm amber sky. A round fruit tree stands on the left with a bird on its branch, rows of seedlings grow in the meadow, and tall foxgloves and sunflowers fill the right side. Gardening tools sit by the seedlings, a butterfly visits the foxgloves, a snail rests on a mossy stone and fruit lies beside a basket under the tree.

Tap an object to start or stop its sound, and drag it to move it. Objects higher in the garden play higher notes, and objects further right repeat faster.
