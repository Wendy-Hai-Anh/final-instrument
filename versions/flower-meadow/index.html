Garden of Sounds

Giant flower meadow

A bright meadow seen from very close to the ground. Giant white, yellow, pink and coral flowers tower on both sides under a clear blue sky, and a mossy log crosses the meadow like a bridge. The snail, a flowerpot and the bird sit on the log, the butterfly floats in the open sky, and the other tools and fruit lie in the grass below.

Every object in this garden is part of one musical instrument. Tap an object to start or stop its song, and drag it to move it; it sings from wherever it is put down. Objects higher up sing higher notes, and objects further right sing faster. All objects keep the same beat and chords, so they make music together. The play button lets the whole garden sing and stops it again, the help button starts a short guide, and the arrow buttons on the sides lead to the other gardens.

Turn your phone sideways to play.
