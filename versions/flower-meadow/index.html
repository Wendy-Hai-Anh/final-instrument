Garden of Sounds

Giant flower meadow

A bright meadow seen from very close to the ground. Giant white, yellow, pink and coral flowers tower on both sides under a clear blue sky, and a mossy log crosses the meadow like a bridge. The snail, a flowerpot and the bird sit on the log, the butterfly floats in the open sky, and the other tools and fruit lie in the grass below.

Tap an object to start or stop its sound, and drag it to move it. Objects higher in the garden play higher notes, and objects further right repeat faster.
